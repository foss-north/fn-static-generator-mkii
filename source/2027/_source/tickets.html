Tickets

Get your ticket via the ticket registration page (requires the setup of an account).

The ticket registration process requires you to register using an OpenID source. At the moment we support GitHub and Google, and as of 2027, our own self-hosted keycloak instance.

If you cannot identify using these services we offer a manual process. This means getting the ticket via mail and processing the payment via bank transfer. Please notice that this process involves a substantial amount of additional work for the organizers so we urge you to consider using the standard process which is quicker and easier. To request a ticket manually, please email us at info@foss-north.se with the subject line "Ticket foss-north {{ confname }}".

Tickets bought before {{ data.general.ticketslunchdate }} include morning sandwich, lunch and coffee. Tickets are still sold until the day before the event, but does not include catering. We have ordered more food than we’ve sold tickets on {{ data.general.ticketslunchdate }}, so we hope to be able to provide food to all participants, but we cannot guarantee this due to lead times ordering supplies.
